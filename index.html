Overview

About the HSC Result 2026

The HSC result is the official outcome of the Higher Secondary Certificate examination, which shows whether a student has passed or failed along with their GPA and subject-wise marks. This year, about 12.70 lakh students appeared in the HSC examination. The written examinations were held from 2 July to 8 August 2026, and the practical examinations concluded by 15 August.

The HSC Result 2026 will be published on 31 October 2026. All 11 education boards will release the result at the same time, so every student receives their result simultaneously.

Students can check their HSC result through four methods — on eboardresults.com, on educationboardresults.gov.bd, by sending an SMS to 16222, or through the official mobile app.

Before result day, students should keep their roll number, registration number, and board name ready. These three details are all you need to check your result in under a minute.

Education board website

HSC Result 2026 from educationboardresults.gov.bd

Both official result portals follow the same simple process. You can check your result on educationboardresults.gov.bd in less than a minute by following these steps:

1
Open the result portal
Open the official result portal: type educationboardresults.gov.bd and press Enter / Go.
2
Select exam & year
On the homepage, you will see an Examination dropdown — select “HSC/Alim/Equivalent” from the list. Then in the Year dropdown, select “2026”
3
Choose your board
Click the Board dropdown menu and select your education board — e.g. Dhaka, Rajshahi, Cumilla, Dinajpur, Chattogram, Barishal, Sylhet, Mymensingh, Jashore, Madrasah, or Technical Board.
4
Enter roll & registration
Keep the Result Type as "Individual Result". Carefully type your Roll Number and Registration Number — both are mandatory.
5
Solve the security key
Type the 4-character security key / captcha code shown on the screen exactly as it appears. Then click the "Get Result" button to see your HSC Result 2026 instantly.
6
View & save your result
Your GPA and grades will appear on the screen instantly. Click Print or press Ctrl+P to print it, or save the page as a PDF for future use.

Due to heavy traffic, the website may load slowly on result day. Wait 1-2 minutes and try refreshing the page, or switch to the second portal. If both sites are busy, use the SMS method below — sending your Roll to 16222 works even when the websites are down.

Board challenge

Rescrunity/Board Challenge Result of HSC 2026

If you have concerns about your HSC Result 2026, you can apply for Board Challenge (Rescrutiny). The education board reviews the marking, totalling, and OMR processing to ensure your result is accurate. The process does not involve re-evaluating or re-examining your answer script. You must submit the application through a Teletalk SIM within the official application period.

Rescrutiny SMS RSC Send to 16222
1
Apply from a Teletalk SIM between 11 and 17 November 2026 — the window usually opens the day after the result.
2
Send RSC to 16222; separate multiple subjects with commas.
3
You get a PIN and fee amount back. Reply RSC YES PIN to confirm.
4
The fee is about Tk 150 per paper, deducted from your Teletalk balance. Most HSC subjects have two papers, so a full subject costs roughly Tk 300.
5
Revised results are published on the board website, usually within 4–6 weeks.

Rescrutiny can raise, lower or keep your grade. Apply only for subjects where you have a genuine reason to expect a change.

Grading

HSC grading scale (GPA)

Every subject grade maps to a grade point. Your GPA is the average across subjects; a top A+ in every subject (with the optional subject bonus) gives the maximum GPA-5.

HSC grading scale: marks range, letter grade and grade point
MarksGradeGrade point
80–100 A+ 5.00
70–79 A 4.00
60–69 A- 3.50
50–59 B 3.00
40–49 C 2.00
33–39 D 1.00
0–32 F 0.00

A grade of F (fail) in any subject means the subject must be improved; GPA is reported once all subjects are passed.

User Query

Frequent Asked Question

When will the HSC Result 2026 be published?

The HSC Result 2026 is expected to be published on 31 October 2026. The official publication date has not yet been confirmed by the education authorities.

How do I check the HSC Result 2026 with marksheet?

Visit eboardresults.com, select HSC, 2026, your education board, and “Individual Result.” Then enter your Roll Number and Registration Number to view the complete marksheet with subject-wise marks, which you can print or save as a PDF.

Can I see the result with only my roll number?

Yes. You can check your GPA using only your Roll Number. However, you will need your Registration Number to access the detailed marksheet with subject-wise marks.

What is the SMS format for HSC Result 2026?

Type HSC 2026 and send it to 16222. For example: HSC DHA 123456 2026. For Alim, type Alim MAD 123456 2026, while Technical (BM/Vocational) students should type HSC TEC 123456 2026.

Can I check the result without a registration number?

No. You need both your Roll Number and Registration Number to check your HSC result and access the complete marksheet with subject-wise marks.

My result is not showing or looks wrong — what should I do?

First, check your board, roll number, and registration number carefully, as a single incorrect digit can prevent the result from loading correctly. If the website is busy, wait a few minutes and try again, or use the SMS method instead. If your GPA still appears incorrect, check the subject-wise grades and consider applying for rescrutiny (board challenge) for the relevant subject.

What was the pass rate for girls and boys in HSC 2025?

Girls achieved a 62.97% pass rate with 37,044 GPA-5s, outperforming boys, who recorded a 54.60% pass rate with 32,053 GPA-5s. This continues the recent trend of girls leading in both the pass rate and number of GPA-5 results.

What if the result website is down?

Traffic is heavy in the first hour. Wait a few minutes and retry, switch between eboardresults.com and educationboardresults.gov.bd, or use SMS — 16222 works even when the sites are slow.

How do I apply for rescrutiny?

From a Teletalk SIM, send RSC to 16222 during the application window, normally 11 to 17 November 2026, then confirm with the PIN you receive. The fee is about Tk 150 per paper.

When are the revised (board challenge) results published?

Rescrutiny results usually come out about 4–6 weeks after the application window closes. They are posted on the same board website, and only the challenged subjects change — a grade can go up, down or stay the same.

What should I do after passing HSC 2026?

University admission is the next step. Public university applications (including the GST cluster test), medical and engineering admissions and National University honours admission all open in the weeks after the result. Keep your roll, registration number and a printed marksheet ready — most application forms ask for your HSC GPA and registration details.

Which board had the best HSC result in 2025?

The Madrasah Education Board led on pass rate at 75.61%. Among the nine general boards, Dhaka was highest at 64.62% and also had by far the most GPA-5 achievers (26,063), while Cumilla was lowest at 48.86%.
